01ControlsJump, double jump, duck, and pause

Jump & double jump

Press Space, ↑, or W. Press again in the air for a second jump. Landing restores both jumps.

Duck & pause

Hold ↓ or S to crouch on the ground. Press P or Esc to pause or resume. Switching away pauses automatically.

Touch controls

Tap the game to jump; tap again to double jump. Hold the DUCK button to crouch. The pause, restart, and PHASE buttons work on every device.

Phase dash

Press X or tap PHASE to pass through hazards for 0.34 seconds. It recharges after five seconds of play and does not use an egg or shield. Use it for an emergency dodge, not for every laser. The button shows the cooldown.

02Revivals & shieldsTwo eggs per run. Hidden-door deaths bypass them.

Every run starts with two Dino Eggs. After an unprotected hit, choose Hatch Again to use one egg, or End This Run. Your score, upgrades, and boss progress stay. Nearby hazards disappear and you get two seconds of protection. Safer Revival can extend that protection.

After using both eggs, another unprotected hit ends the run. A shield blocks a hit without using an egg. Restarting begins a fresh run with two eggs and no upgrades.

03CollectiblesWhat every pickup does

Shield inside a square

Blocks one hit. Expires after 10 seconds unless Longer Shield extends it. The dotted border around your dino means a shield is active.

Clock inside a square

Slows the world for seven seconds. Your jumping stays responsive. Longer Slow Motion extends the timer.

Bone / fossil

Gives 25 points. Valuable Fossils raises that value; High Road Treasure adds points when you collect a fossil on an upper route.

Cracked eggshell

Collect three fragments to build a one-hit shield. Easy Shell Repair lowers the requirement to two. Fragments do not grant extra revivals.

04Upgrade choicesAfter bosses 2, 4, 6, and 8. Three random cards.

Defeat two bosses to get an upgrade choice, then defeat two more for another. That means picks after bosses 2 and 4. Biome changes do not open the upgrade menu.

The game pauses while you choose. Three different upgrades are randomly drawn from the nine below. Click a card or press 1, 2, or 3. You can also skip. Each card tells you exactly what changes, including the current and new value.

Your equipped upgrades appear above the game. Repeated picks improve a perk up to its cap; maxed-out perks stop appearing. Upgrades survive revivals and reset when you start a new run.

Longer Shield

Adds two seconds to shield pickup duration per level: 10 → 12 → 14 → 16 → 18 seconds. Still blocks only one hit.

05World, routes & bossesMilestones, events, and the ten encounters

The score includes distance and collectible bonuses. Every 3,000 points brings a new biome, scenery, and obstacles. The final approach stops adding obstacles and routes so the runway clears naturally. A border announcement previews the next biome; existing obstacles keep scrolling with their original appearance while the background fades. There are 55 named biomes in shuffled order after the starting Desert, followed by generated frontier regions.

The high road

Double jump onto dotted platforms for fossils and fragments. Scenic routes have wider ledges; daring routes may include a bird. Staying on the ground is always an option.

Themed terrain

Normal biomes have lighter terrain passages: broken ground on coasts and dunes, lava in volcanic regions, roots and vines in forests, crystal gates in caves and ruins, geysers in snow and fungal lands, and meteor impacts in canyons. Rare variants chain several hazards. A clean passage earns +25 points normally or +60 in a mutation. Eruptions show a dashed footprint and a countdown before activating. Void and lava falls bypass shields and phase; a main-run fall costs a revival. Terrain clears naturally before bosses and biome borders. Slow motion may require a double jump over a wider gap.

World events

Snow squalls, meteor showers, mini stampedes, and fossil-rich firefly trails last six seconds. Boss encounters clear the event.

Achievement map

Open Achievements from the home screen. Six branches hold 48 goals, including exploration, collecting, builds, clean wins, and comebacks. Select any node to see its exact goal, progress, and prerequisites. Progress saves across runs in this browser.

The five bosses

  1. Bird · about 11 seconds. Six random multi-ball patterns, varied shot spacing and warning delays. Jump low trains and crouch under high ones. Ceiling shots make careless double jumps risky.
  2. Prism Triceratops · about 12 seconds. A scanning line settles into a locked laser. Read the final line: crouch for head-height beams, jump low curtains, double jump tall ones. Its randomized bag also includes scatter beams.
  3. Quarry T. rex · about 13 seconds. Single magnets, twin magnets, and tall towers arrive in random order. Jump both low twins together, then duck their returning head-height rocks. Towers require a double jump. The next formation waits until recall clears you.
  4. Volcanic Dragon · about 14 seconds. Three arena puzzles appear in shuffled order. Rift Cage raises a low stone between lava and a ceiling: use one jump. High Tide raises a much higher stone: use a double jump. Ash Seal fills the upper arena: land and crouch. Stone positions, widths, heights and lifetimes vary. Stones crumble after landing; each pressure wave ends before the next begins.
  5. Shadow Dino · about 15 seconds. Mirror shots approach from a random side, then echo from the opposite side. Jump both low shots together; duck both high shots. Mirror curtains require double jumps. Attack order and return spacing vary.

The five new endgame bosses

  1. 8,000 · Beat Reaper · about 16 seconds. A fast rhythm gauntlet with paired spike beats. One short jump per beat.
  2. 12,000 · Press Sovereign · about 17 seconds. An assembly line of sudden crushers and moving bone spikes. Alternate crouches and jumps.
  3. 16,000 · Parallax Moth · about 18 seconds. Transform into a UFO and tap through narrow, shifting crystal windows.
  4. 20,000 · Ion Leviathan · about 19 seconds. Hold/release ship flight through moving meteor rotors, at a higher scroll speed.
  5. 25,000 · Null Architect · about 21 seconds. A jetpack duel with shifting bulkheads. Hold for more height, release to drop and refuel. This ends the ten-boss campaign; Explore On continues afterward.

The five mechanical bosses use progressively faster arenas and tighter patterns. Bosses appear at their score milestones after the previous encounter and recovery window. Fight lengths and layouts vary. Upgrade choices continue after bosses 6 and 8.

Watch the attack warnings. Survive the countdown to win each fight. Reviving preserves the boss timer and clears its active projectiles.

06Mutations & mechanical modesDouble points, concealed traps, and new controls

Every mutation awards 2× distance points. Fossils pay 50 points before upgrades; portal completion adds 450–650 points and a museum piece. Your portal's scrolling speed is exactly the speed you were moving at when you entered, including an active slow-motion effect. It stays fixed for the trial. Main-world mutations also preserve entry speed.

In Normal mode, hidden-door death ends the entire run. Eggs and shields cannot save a portal run. Main-world mutations retain the usual two revivals. There are sudden crushers, concealed spikes, moving traps, almost invisible walls, and islands that collapse after landing. Hidden traps reveal earlier now: about 0.85–0.95 seconds before arrival. Once revealed, that obstacle stays visible until it scrolls off screen.

Runner & rhythm controls

Space / ↑ / W / tap jumps. Normal runner stages allow a second jump. Rhythm mode uses one shorter jump per beat: watch the beat bar and sync your taps. Hold ↓ to crouch under crushers or vines.

UFO controls

Tap jump to flap upward. Let the UFO fall between taps. Moving crystal windows require a changing cadence rather than holding the button.

Ship controls

Hold Space / ↑ / W, hold the canvas, or hold BOOST to rise. Release to descend. Thrust has inertia; steer toward the next opening rather than chasing the current one.

Jetpack controls

Holding the jump button extends your ascent dynamically. Release to fall and recharge the fuel meter. Down increases descent. Longer holds fly higher but use more fuel. The final boss uses this mode.

Secret rewards & museum

Survive a randomized 15–18 seconds, collecting optional square fossils along the way. Main-world time freezes during a portal visit. Find unique museum parts in fossils or earn a guaranteed new one from a successful trial. Visit the museum ↗

The longer frontier

Seven new normal regions join the pool: Iron Railyard, Tidal Archipelago, Scarlet Badlands, Hollow Roots, Glass Steppe, Honeycomb Highlands, and Midnight Orchard. The 55-region shuffled pool covers the path through 25,000 points without repeating a main-world region.

07Find the nine secret biomesDistinct rules, lethal surprises, and 2× points

Every run starts in the plain Desert. Climb the early HIDDEN STAIRS and touch its gate, or accept the traveler's invitation. Each portal offers three named destinations from the nine below. Later world regions may also mutate. Mutation rolls use shuffled bags so the same mutation does not keep appearing.

Block Void

COLLAPSING ISLANDS · 2× POINTS

Narrow floating stones break away shortly after you land. Restore jumps on each island, then leave before it falls. Void falls are fatal.

Boneworks

CRUSHER ASSEMBLY LINE · 2× POINTS

Bone presses slam down without alarms. Crouch beneath the press, then jump the moving bone spikes that follow.

Watchful Woods

HIDDEN THORNS · 2× POINTS

Ground thorns stay concealed until you approach. Hanging vines move vertically and some spikes slide sideways. Empty ground cannot be trusted.

Crystal Echo

UFO FLIGHT · 2× POINTS

Your dinosaur becomes a tap-controlled UFO. Change your flap rhythm to pass through oscillating crystal windows.

Starfall

SHIP FLIGHT · 2× POINTS

Hold to climb and release to descend. Meteor rotor openings move while your ship carries momentum, so plan your next arc early.

Lava Caldera

SPRING-POWERED LONG JUMPS · 2× POINTS

Automatic springs launch you across lava rivers that ordinary jumps cannot clear. Land before the hidden volcano on the next bank.

Clockwork Circuit

RHYTHM RUNNER · 2× POINTS

One short jump per beat, with a visual timing bar. The machine varies its tempo between stages and can add paired spike beats.

Phantom Foundry

ALMOST INVISIBLE WALLS · 2× POINTS

Ghost walls are barely visible before becoming solid. Other sections replace them with sudden crushing ceilings. React to the actual shape, not empty-looking space.

Gravity Rift

FUEL-LIMITED JETPACK · 2× POINTS

Hold for a longer ascent, release to recharge, and steer through shifting bulkheads. Fuel and height both matter.

08No clip & practiceLearn the same map with unlimited attempts

Choose your Run Mode above the game. You can change it during a run; the last choice is remembered for the next visit.

No clip · never die

Hazards cannot end the run or spend eggs. The game flashes a soft red tint and counts each would-be death above the runway. One obstacle counts once while you pass through it. Falls recover onto a ghost bridge; flight boundaries clamp your position so you can continue steering.

Practice · continue here

Death pauses the game instead of ending it. Choose Continue Here or press Space to recover just past the trap, keeping your score, current world, and boss timer. You get a short recovery grace period and unlimited attempts, including inside hidden doors.

Retry the same checkpoint

Retry Checkpoint restores the recent saved map, player position, elapsed time, and random generator state. The section does not reroll. Safe checkpoints save automatically. Press C or Save Checkpoint to pin a manual checkpoint until you continue past a death or enter a new encounter.

Training progress

No clip and Practice scores, museum rewards, and achievements are kept separate from normal records. If assistance was enabled at any time, restart in Normal mode for a run that saves normal progress.
