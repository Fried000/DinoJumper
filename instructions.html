01ControlsJump, double jump, duck, and pause

Jump & double jump

Press Space, ↑, or W. Press again in the air for a second jump. Landing restores both jumps.

Duck & pause

Hold ↓ or S to crouch on the ground. Press P or Esc to pause or resume. Switching away pauses automatically.

Touch controls

Tap the game to jump; tap again to double jump. Hold the DUCK button to crouch. The pause, restart, and PHASE buttons work on every device.

Phase dash

Press X or tap PHASE to pass through hazards for 0.34 seconds. It recharges after five seconds of play and does not use an egg or shield. Use it for an emergency dodge, not for every laser. The button shows the cooldown.

02Revivals & shieldsTwo eggs per run. Know what protects you.

Every run starts with two Dino Eggs. After an unprotected hit, choose Hatch Again to use one egg, or End This Run. Your score, upgrades, and boss progress stay. Nearby hazards disappear and you get two seconds of protection. Safer Revival can extend that protection.

After using both eggs, another unprotected hit ends the run. A shield blocks a hit without using an egg. Restarting begins a fresh run with two eggs and no upgrades.

03CollectiblesWhat every pickup does

Shield inside a square

Blocks one hit. Expires after 10 seconds unless Longer Shield extends it. The dotted border around your dino means a shield is active.

Clock inside a square

Slows the world for seven seconds. Your jumping stays responsive. Longer Slow Motion extends the timer.

Bone / fossil

Gives 25 points. Valuable Fossils raises that value; High Road Treasure adds points when you collect a fossil on an upper route.

Cracked eggshell

Collect three fragments to build a one-hit shield. Easy Shell Repair lowers the requirement to two. Fragments do not grant extra revivals.

04Upgrade choicesAfter bosses 2 and 4. Three random cards.

Defeat two bosses to get an upgrade choice, then defeat two more for another. That means picks after bosses 2 and 4. Biome changes do not open the upgrade menu.

The game pauses while you choose. Three different upgrades are randomly drawn from the nine below. Click a card or press 1, 2, or 3. You can also skip. Each card tells you exactly what changes, including the current and new value.

Your equipped upgrades appear above the game. Repeated picks improve a perk up to its cap; maxed-out perks stop appearing. Upgrades survive revivals and reset when you start a new run.

Longer Shield

Adds two seconds to shield pickup duration per level: 10 → 12 → 14 → 16 → 18 seconds. Still blocks only one hit.

05World, routes & bossesMilestones, events, and the five encounters

The score includes distance and collectible bonuses. Every 3,000 points brings a new biome, scenery, and obstacles. The final approach stops adding obstacles and routes so the runway clears naturally. A border announcement previews the next biome; existing obstacles keep scrolling with their original appearance while the background fades. There are 48 named biomes, followed by generated frontier regions.

The high road

Double jump onto dotted platforms for fossils and fragments. Scenic routes have wider ledges; daring routes may include a bird. Staying on the ground is always an option.

World events

Snow squalls, meteor showers, mini stampedes, and fossil-rich firefly trails last six seconds. Boss encounters clear the event.

Achievement map

Open Achievements from the home screen. Six branches hold 48 goals, including exploration, collecting, builds, clean wins, and comebacks. Select any node to see its exact goal, progress, and prerequisites. Progress saves across runs in this browser.

The five bosses

  1. 1,000 points · Bird · 7 seconds. Random ball trains, pairs, and scatter shots. Jump low balls, duck high balls, and watch the ceiling.
  2. 2,000 points · Triceratops · 7.5 seconds. Prism Triceratops fires warned lasers at continuously randomized heights. Thin head-height beams need a crouch; low curtains need a jump; tall curtains need a double jump. Scatter shots can cross any height. Your first upgrade pick follows victory.
  3. 3,000 points · T. rex · 8 seconds. Quarry T. rex alternates magnetic rocks and tall towers. Jump a magnetic rock, then duck when it pauses behind you and returns at head height. Double jump the towers. The next formation waits until the returning rock clears you.
  4. 4,000 points · Dragon · 8.5 seconds. The dragon reshapes the arena: a warned lava floor raises a safe stone beneath you; jump onto it. Then the stone disappears before an ash ceiling descends: land and crouch on the ground. Attacks never overlap. Your second upgrade pick follows victory.
  5. 5,000 points · Shadow Dino · 9 seconds. Stacked volleys and delayed echoes. Survive to beat the gauntlet, then choose Explore On for endless biomes.

Watch the attack warnings. Survive the countdown to win each fight. Reviving preserves the boss timer and clears its active projectiles.

06Detours & discoveriesRare worlds, portals, fossil trails, and your museum

Fossil trails

Optional five-fossil arcs follow your normal jump. Collect the whole trail for +100 extra points. Miss one and keep the fossils you did collect. Watch the trail counter above the runway.

Rare biome mutations

Some visits reveal Moon Mirage, Fossil Bloom, Watchful Woods, Crystal Echo, Starfall, or Ember Garden. These change the scenery and palette. Gravity, running speed, and enemy difficulty stay familiar.

The traveler

A scarf-wearing dinosaur occasionally offers a portal after the runway clears. Your run pauses: choose Enter Portal (E) or Keep Running (Esc). Neither choice costs an egg.

Hidden gates

Some upper routes hide a glowing rectangular gate. Touch it to reveal the same optional secret-biome challenge. Stay on the ground if you want to skip it.

Portal challenge

Follow four jumping arcs and collect at least 8 of 12 fossils in nine seconds. No enemies inside. Your main-world distance, hazards, power-up timers, and boss milestones freeze. Success gives +250 points, a shield, and a guaranteed undiscovered museum part until your collection is complete. A missed target returns you safely without spending an egg.

Your fossil museum

Ordinary fossils have an 8% chance to reveal a new skeleton piece. Portal wins guarantee one. Assemble skull, spine, limbs, and tail for four creatures. Museum pieces never duplicate and save between runs in this browser. Visit your museum ↗

Sound & boss personality

Single and double jumps have different quiet arcade sounds. Use Sound On/Off above the game; your preference saves. Boss taunts and cracked dragon armor are visual reactions, with no extra attacks or surprise acceleration.
